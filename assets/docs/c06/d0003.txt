[文稿]

福岡是日本九州最大又熱鬧的城市。Fukuoka is the biggest and busiest city in Kyushu, Japan.

從台灣搭飛機只要兩個多小時就能到福岡。It only takes about two hours to fly from Taiwan to Fukuoka.

福岡最有名的美食是豚骨拉麵。Fukuoka is famous for its tonkotsu (pork bone) ramen.

很多同學喜歡的一蘭拉麵就是從福岡來的！Ichiran Ramen, which many students love, is from Fukuoka!

在福岡可以去「拉麵街」吃各種拉麵口味。You can go to “Ramen Street” in Fukuoka to try many ramen flavors.

福岡有一座古老又有歷史的福岡城。Fukuoka has an old and historical castle called Fukuoka Castle.

走進城堡，就像走進忍者和武士的世界。Walking into the castle feels like entering the world of ninjas and samurai.

「太宰府天滿宮」是學生最愛去的神社。Dazaifu Tenmangu is a favorite shrine for students.

大家會去那裡祈求考試順利、學業進步。People go there to pray for good grades and success in exams.

那邊的梅枝餅又香又甜，像是日式紅豆餅。Umegae mochi there is sweet and yummy, like a Japanese red bean cake.

福岡也有超酷的水族館「海之中道」。Fukuoka also has a cool aquarium called Uminonakamichi.

裡面可以看到海豚表演，超級精彩！You can watch exciting dolphin shows inside!

還有很多可愛的海洋生物，像小丑魚和企鵝。There are many cute sea animals like clownfish and penguins.

喜歡逛街的話，一定要去天神商圈。If you like shopping, you must visit the Tenjin area.

那裡有很多文具、零食、動漫周邊可以買。You can buy lots of stationery, snacks, and anime goods there.

福岡是一個好玩、好吃又值得一去的城市！Fukuoka is a fun, tasty, and must-visit city!
