[文稿]

小樂是一個活潑好動的小男孩，最喜歡在公園裡跳繩。Leo is an energetic little boy who loves jumping rope in the park.

每天放學後，他都會帶著跳繩，和朋友們一起挑戰各種跳法。Every day after school, he brings his rope and tries different jumping styles with his friends.

跳繩讓他跑得更快，體力也變得越來越好。Jumping rope makes him run faster and builds up his stamina.

體育課時，小樂總是第一個完成熱身運動。In P.E. class, Leo is always the first to finish the warm-up.

媽媽說，跳繩能讓心臟更健康，小樂聽了好開心。Mom says jumping rope is good for the heart, and Leo is happy to hear that.

他發現，常常跳繩後，晚上睡得更香了。He discovered that after jumping rope, he sleeps more soundly at night.

有一次，小樂挑戰雙腳交叉跳，雖然跌倒了，但他不氣餒。One time, Leo tried a criss-cross jump. He fell down but didn’t give up.

他學會了：跳繩讓他變得更有耐心和勇氣。He learned that jumping rope makes him more patient and brave.

不久後，他還會雙飛繩，一次跳兩下，朋友們都鼓掌叫好。Soon, he could do double unders, jumping twice in one turn, and his friends clapped for him.

老師說，小樂的身體協調變得超棒，是跳繩幫了大忙。The teacher said Leo’s coordination has improved a lot thanks to jumping rope.

小樂的注意力也變得更集中，課堂表現大大進步。Leo’s focus has improved too, and he’s doing better in class.

跳繩時，他和朋友們互相加油，學會了團隊合作。While jumping rope, he and his friends cheer each other on and learn teamwork.

每次跳完繩，臉紅紅的他都笑得像太陽一樣燦爛。After every jump, his cheeks turn red and he smiles like sunshine.

他還帶著小妹妹一起跳，妹妹也越來越喜歡運動了。He even taught his little sister to jump rope, and she started loving exercise too.

小樂說，跳繩像一首歌，節奏讓人快樂又健康。Leo says jumping rope is like a song—its rhythm makes you happy and healthy.

最後，小樂立志要參加跳繩比賽，讓更多人發現跳繩的魔法！In the end, Leo decides to join a jump rope contest and share its magic with everyone!
