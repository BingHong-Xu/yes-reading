[文稿]

小熊是一個四年級的小學生，最喜歡用平板玩遊戲和看影片。Little Bear is a fourth-grade student. He loves to play games and watch videos on his tablet.

有一天，他在網路上看到一個網站叫做「創意私房」，上面寫著：「這裡有超刺激的內容！」One day, he saw a website called "Creative Secret." It said, “Exciting things are here!”

小熊點進去，畫面出現了一些奇怪又讓人不舒服的影片。Little Bear clicked it. Strange and scary videos appeared.

他嚇了一跳，心裡覺得怪怪的，但又有點好奇。He was shocked. It felt wrong, but he was a little curious.

網站說：「只要上傳朋友的祕密影片，就能看到更多。」The website said, “Upload your friend’s secret video to see more!”

小熊想起朋友洗澡時不小心被拍到的影片，網站說可以用來換東西。Little Bear thought about a video of his friend. The site said it could be used to get things.

他猶豫了，但心裡有點擔心這樣做是不是對的。He felt unsure. He was worried if this was right.

放學後，他去找了學校的輔導老師，偷偷說了這件事。After school, he told the school counselor about it.

老師皺著眉頭說：「這是壞人設的陷阱，那個網站是犯法的地方！」The teacher frowned. “That is a trap by bad people. That website is illegal!”

老師告訴小熊，那些影片都是偷偷拍的，裡面的人根本沒同意被分享。The teacher said the videos were taken secretly. People didn’t say yes to share them.

小熊問：「那如果我不小心點到了怎麼辦？」Little Bear asked, “What if I clicked it by mistake?”

老師說：「遇到這種網站，要馬上關掉，告訴爸爸媽媽或老師。」The teacher said, “Close it right away. Tell your parents or teacher.”

老師還說：「永遠不要拍或分享別人祕密的照片或影片，這樣會傷害到別人。」The teacher added, “Never take or share secret pictures or videos. It can hurt others.”

小熊點點頭，決定以後上網要更小心。Little Bear nodded. He decided to be more careful online.

他也提醒朋友：「有些網站是陷阱，不能亂看亂傳！」He told his friends, “Some websites are traps. Don’t click or share!”

從此以後，小熊學會了保護自己，也成了同學們的網路小偵探！From then on, Little Bear learned to protect himself. He became a smart internet helper at school!
